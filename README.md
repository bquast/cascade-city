# Cascade City v7 — "Country Life"

The living-countryside + fidelity round. 4km x 4km, vanilla three.js, no build step.

## New in v7
- **Never stranded again**: dying respawns you at the NEAREST settlement, and country
  roads now carry real traffic — pickups, sedans, even tractors trundling between
  towns. Flag one down (or just take it).
- **Planes**: a drivable Duster crop plane. W to full throttle down the runway and
  it lifts off; steer with A/D, hold W to climb, Space to dive, S to brake. Land
  gently (glide in, nose up) or eat 40 damage. Cascade Intl (tower, hangar, marked
  runway) + 3 dirt strips: Dusty Airfield, Hollow Strip, Crop Strip. A parked plane
  waits at each, and one AI plane circles the island all day.
- **Boats**: drivable Skiffs at the pier, the lake, and the port. They float, bob,
  and beach themselves if you drive ashore. AI boats cruise offshore.
- **Farms**: four working farms — barns, silos, crop rows, hay bales, fences —
  with parked tractors, Rancheros, and a combine harvester (drivable, majestic,
  slow). The ranch out east runs CATTLE, herded by COWBOYS ON HORSEBACK.
- **Birds**: woodpeckers hammering on trunks (red caps, listen for the tilt) and
  sparrows in the crowns. Walk up and they burst away to another tree.
- **Country folk**: hillbillies around Hickory Corner, vaqueros working the farms,
  rednecks at Dusty Palms — each with their own look and hats.
- **Desert dressing**: junked barrels, tires, planks, lone trailers, and rolling
  tumbleweeds.
- **Fidelity pass**: chamfered car bodies with real glass, mirrors, bumpers and
  hubcaps (~20x the triangles); round-headed, cylinder-limbed pedestrians; lit
  window grids on every downtown tower (glorious at night); soft shadows.
- **City form**: way more cul-de-sacs, curved crescent lanes inside residential
  blocks, and CASCADE HEIGHTS — a winding mansion road (pools included) climbing
  the foothill. Windy streets are player roads; AI sticks to the grid + highways.

## Controls
WASD · mouse aim · E enter/exit · Q switch character · T missions · R radio ·
H horn · Space handbrake/dive · 1/2/3 weapons · Shift sprint

## Deploy
Drag the folder into Cloudflare Pages. Build: none. Output: `/`.
